Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This starter policy explains general privacy considerations and should be updated to match the site's actual code, hosting, and data practices.

Information You Enter

The Resume Maker may ask for personal details. The Photo Editor lets you select a photo. Avoid entering information you do not want included in your output.

Browser and Third-Party Services

The tools are designed for browser use, but this does not guarantee every resource is offline or that no data is transmitted. The homepage loads Tailwind CSS from a third-party CDN, which receives technical connection information when requested by your browser.

Storage and Sharing

Actual data handling depends on the tool code, browser, hosting, and third-party services. Do not assume information is never stored or transmitted without reviewing the implementation.

Updates and Questions

Update this policy if you add analytics, advertising, accounts, or server uploads. For questions, visit Contact Us.

This is a starter policy, not legal advice. Review it for your actual data practices and applicable laws before publication.
